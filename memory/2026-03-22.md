### Technical Insights

- **Methodology**: Wavelet Analysis (time-frequency domain)
  - Bivariate Wavelet Coherence: Local correlation between variables at specific time scales and frequencies
  - Partial Wavelet Coherence: Controls for third variable (CVI) to assess net correlation
  - Multiple Wavelet Correlation: Overall linear association strength
  - Multiple Wavelet Cross-Correlation: Identifies leading/lagging variables
