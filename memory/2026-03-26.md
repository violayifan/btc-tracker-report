## Circle Business Model Analysis

User requested analysis of Circle's盈利模式 (profit model). Key findings:

### Core Revenue Streams
1. **USDC Reserve Investment (Primary)** - 100% fiat reserves invested in short-term US Treasuries, generating significant yield in high-rate environment
2. **Transaction Fees** - Minor fees on USDC minting/redemption
3. **Bank Deposit Interest** - Interest on reserves held in partner banks
4. **Cross-border Payment Services** - Enterprise cross-border payment solution with fees
5. **Account Service Fees** - Enterprise account management, custody, API access fees

### Business Model Characteristics
- **Asset-backed**: USDC 100% fiat reserves, transparent and secure
- **Interest Arbitrage**: Leverage high-rate environment to increase profits
- **Scale Effects**: Larger USDC circulation = higher returns
- **Compliance Advantage**: Regulated stablecoin, high enterprise trust

### Growth Drivers
1. Interest rate environment (Fed hikes increase Treasury yields)
2. USDC growth (from hundreds of millions to 50B+ USD circulation)
3. Institutional adoption (more enterprises using USDC for cross-border payments)
4. New products (Web3 service wallets, etc.)
